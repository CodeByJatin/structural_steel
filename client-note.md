**SUBJECT:** Practical Material Yield and Drawing Revision Pilot Program  

---

### Executive Summary
Our business fabricates **80,000 tonnes of structural steel each year**. This represents an annual material cost of approximately **$76 million**. Our plate steel scrap is currently reported at **20% to 25%**, and late drawing revisions from clients create unbilled scrap.

We propose a practical **12-week pilot project**. It connects to our existing **Strumis system** to reuse leftover steel and recover revision scrap costs. A conservative **1.0% waste reduction** plus recovered client revision claims will save an estimated **$950,000 per year**. The program pays for itself in less than two months.

---

### 1. What We Propose to Do
We do not replace our software. We do not disrupt factory cutting. We implement three practical tools:

1. **Reuse Leftover Steel Plates (Remnants):**  
   When a job finishes cutting, large usable steel pieces remain. Our software tracks these remnants and matches them to upcoming jobs before we buy new steel plates.
2. **Combine Project Orders on Shared Plates:**  
   Instead of cutting one project at a time, the software groups orders of identical steel grade across active projects. Small parts fill empty gaps between large parts, reducing edge scrap.
3. **Bill Clients for Design Changes:**  
   When client architects change drawings after cutting begins, the software identifies the change and drafts a commercial claim so the client pays for the scrap steel.
4. **Human Workers Keep Full Control:**  
   Our nesting engineers maintain **100% sign-off authority** on every cutting plan. The software proposes solutions; the human engineer makes the final decision.

### 2. What We Need from Our Team
* **IT Team (Week 1):** Provide a read-only data export from Strumis (no system changes).

* **Yard Crew (Week 7):** Place barcode stickers on usable offcuts and put them on standard racks.
